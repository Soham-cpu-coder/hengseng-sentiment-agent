Honest Quantitative Limitations & Disclosures

1. Asymmetric Alternative Data Window:
Social sentiment from Apify (Telegram, LIHKG, Naver 종목토론실) and Exa news covers a recent 10-day lookback window, while daily financial bars span over 120 trading days. Days prior to the active 10-day window lack real social commentary and are modeled with explicit missingness indicators (has_social=0) rather than synthetic backward extrapolation.


2. Retail Noise and Attention Cascades:
Social forums exhibit significant herd dynamics, emotional retail trading bias, and occasional promotional spam. While log-scale follower weighting and engagement metrics mitigate outliers, sentiment shifts can be noisy leading indicators.


3. Out-of-Sample Statistical Power & Sample Size:
Due to daily bar frequency and expanding walk-forward splits, the number of out-of-sample test samples is modest. Statistical significance (t-statistics / p-values on Information Coefficients) is limited, and users should exercise caution before deploying capital based solely on short-term sentiment signals.


4. Execution Frictions Not Modeled & No Claim of Profitability:
Reported strategy Sharpe ratios and cumulative returns reflect theoretical gross signals and do not deduct exchange transaction levies, stamp duties (such as HKEX 0.1% stamp duty or KRX 0.18% transaction tax), bid-ask spread crossing, or short-borrow financing fees. No claim of trading profitability is made unless substantiated by verified live execution.


5. Disciplined Algorithmic Filtering:
Positions default to HOLD whenever the expected risk/reward ratio is below 1.5 or conviction is below 0.35, protecting capital against adverse risk-asymmetry.
